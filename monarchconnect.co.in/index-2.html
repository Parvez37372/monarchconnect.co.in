01 / ABOUT

What Monarch Does.

Monarch Connect is a platform that brings together people who want to build and the professionals who build. Homeowners and consumers discover trusted builders and construction companies. Builders and companies reach people who are ready to start.

Every connection begins with verification, so trust comes before the first conversation.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

The Problem
We're Solving.

Building is one of the biggest decisions a family or business will make, yet finding the right builder still depends on referrals, guesswork and promises that are hard to verify.

For those who want to build

It is difficult to know who is genuinely reliable. Quality varies, track records are hard to see, and the cost of a wrong choice is high.

For those who build

Good work often goes unseen. Skilled builders and honest companies need a better way to reach serious customers and demonstrate their expertise.

The result is a market where trust is scarce and talent is hard to find, on both sides.
