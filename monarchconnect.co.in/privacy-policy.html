Privacy Policy

At Monarch Connect, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when you visit, communicate with or use our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it may be protected and the choices available to you.

Last Updated: 07 October 2026

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Monarch Connect website, related pages, online forms, account areas and other digital interactions operated by or on behalf of Monarch Connect.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue use of the website.

02 Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Monarch Connect, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us. This may include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Business or organization information.
  • Information submitted through contact or enquiry forms.
  • Account or login-related information where applicable.
  • Information you provide when communicating with us.
  • Any other information you voluntarily choose to provide.

03 Automatically Collected Information

When you access our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by standard web technologies. This information may include browser type, device type, operating system, approximate usage information, pages visited and general technical information.

Such information may help us understand website performance, identify technical problems and improve the user experience.

04 How We Use Information

Information collected through the website may be used for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.
  • Providing information about our services.
  • Managing accounts and authorized access.
  • Communicating with users regarding their requests.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Monitoring technical performance and resolving errors.
  • Complying with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

05 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Monarch Connect may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand usage patterns or improve website performance.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device by a website. Depending on your browser settings, you may be able to block or delete cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06 Account and Login Information

If Monarch Connect provides account or login functionality, certain information may be required to create and maintain your account.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for notifying us if you believe that unauthorized access to your account has occurred.

We may use account information to authenticate users, maintain account security, provide authorized features and manage services associated with an account.

07 Communication

If you contact Monarch Connect by email, contact form, telephone or another communication method, we may retain relevant information from that communication in order to respond to your request and maintain appropriate business records.

08 Sharing of Information

Monarch Connect does not intend to sell or rent your personal information for monetary gain.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary for legitimate business operations, technical service providers, website hosting, security, communication services, legal obligations or other authorized purposes.

Where third-party service providers are used, they may process information only as necessary to provide the relevant service or fulfill an authorized purpose.

09 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, website, server or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect information, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10 Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or comply with applicable legal requirements.

11 Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services. These third parties may have their own privacy policies and practices.

Monarch Connect is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

12 Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate steps can be considered.

13 Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the nature of the information, you may have rights or choices relating to your personal information.

You may contact Monarch Connect to request information about personal information we may hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information or raise a privacy-related concern.

14 Changes to This Privacy Policy

Monarch Connect may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be published on this page and the updated date will be changed accordingly.

15 Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable laws of India, subject to any mandatory rights or protections available to users under applicable law.

Privacy Questions?

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact Monarch Connect through the contact information available on our website.

We will make reasonable efforts to review and respond to privacy-related enquiries.
